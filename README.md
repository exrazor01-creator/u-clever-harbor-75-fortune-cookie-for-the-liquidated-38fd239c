One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated — the fortune that makes them laugh instead of cry.

## The entry

> The stars say your fortune is rising. Your liquidation respectfully disagrees.

78 characters, 11 words, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–160 character and 20-word gates.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **A fortune for a liquidated trader** — the cookie voice ("The stars say...") and the trading loss ("liquidation") are both in it.
2. **Laugh, not tears** — the joke lands on the loss and on the trader, rueful and warm: the loss itself is polite, never cruel and never smug.
3. **Reads whole in a single breath** — one line, 11 words, the way a real fortune reads.
4. **Fresh** — a double meaning on "fortune" (luck and wealth at once) and a surprising last word: the liquidation answers back, instead of the obvious liquidation pun.
5. **Keeps the fortune voice** — small, oracular, slightly formal phrasing ("respectfully disagrees") right up to the punchline that breaks it.
